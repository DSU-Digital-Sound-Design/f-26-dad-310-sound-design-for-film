Five graded assignments build the craft: live Foley design, recorded Foley,
field recording, editing and processing, and ambience building. The final
project brings these skills together under picture.

Assignment 5: Sound Synthesis has been dropped and will not count toward your
grade. Ambiences keeps its original number, Assignment 6, and is due November 30.

Every assignment is played out loud in class. Make sure you're comfortable
sharing what you've made with the group.
