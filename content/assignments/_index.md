Six assignments build the craft in order — designing foley, recording it,
capturing your own field material, editing and processing it, synthesising what
can't be recorded, and building ambiences — before the final project asks you to
put all of it under picture. The editing exercise runs early, alongside the
REAPER review.

Every assignment is played out loud in class. Make sure you're comfortable
sharing what you've made with the group.
