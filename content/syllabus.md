## Course

DAD 310 — Sound Design for Film. 3 credits. Fall 2026, Dakota State University.
Prerequisites: DAD 222 and MUS 204.

This course is an introduction to sound design for film and linear media.
Students examine and analyze the use of music, dialogue, and sound effects for
narrative storytelling. Topics include microphone techniques, foley, field
recording, sound design, and mixing.

## Meetings

## Instructor

Tate Carson · TCB 305 · <tate.carson@dsu.edu>

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

1. Critically analyze a film with respect to its aesthetic and technical sound components.
2. Plan and create an original sound design for a short film.
3. Record original sound effects, backgrounds, and voice recordings.
4. Identify the roles and responsibilities of the sound designer in a professional film.

## Materials

No required textbook. Studio-quality headphones are required and available to
rent if you need a pair. We work in REAPER, with Vital for synthesis and SoundQ
and Freesound for library material.
