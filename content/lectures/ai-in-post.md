Two AI technologies bear directly on the jobs this course trains for.

Dialogue isolation strips noise from a voice recording far beyond what ReaFIR
can do. iZotope RX Dialogue Isolate and Waves Clarity Vx are the
post-production standards, and free web versions exist: Adobe's Enhance Speech
and the ElevenLabs Voice Isolator. This extends the repair ladder from the
[dialogue editing lesson](/lectures/dialogue-editing/): lines that once needed
ADR can now sometimes be saved.

Text-to-SFX generators produce sound effects from a written prompt.

## Discussion

- If isolation keeps improving, what happens to ADR sessions? To the alternate
  take habit?
- Foley is performance: timing, weight, character in a footstep. Which parts of
  that could a generator reproduce? Which parts can't it?
- Who recorded the sounds these generators learned from, and were they asked?
- The [syllabus](/syllabus/) draws a line between processing your own
  recordings and generating material. Which side does dialogue isolation fall
  on? Which side does text-to-SFX fall on?
